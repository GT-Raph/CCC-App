Delete your CCC account

The CCC app is published by Calvary Charismatic Centre. You can delete your account at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open the CCC app and log in.
  2. Tap your initial at the top right to open your account.
  3. Tap Delete account, read what will be deleted, then tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email [church email address] from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my CCC account”. We’ll delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What’s deleted

  • Your name, email address, password and branch
  • The details you gave about yourself: date of birth, gender, marital status and whether you’re a student
  • Your ministry memberships and requests to join ministries
  • Any staff, ministry leader or media leader role you have

What’s kept

  • News, events, sermons and photos that church staff posted for the church. They belong to the church, and its staff can remove them.
  • Copies in our service provider’s routine backups, until those backups expire.
Questions? Email [church email address]. Read our privacy policy.
