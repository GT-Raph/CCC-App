CCC app privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

The CCC app is published by Calvary Charismatic Centre (“the church”, “we”). This page explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted. Questions go to [church email address].

What we collect and why

Your account

Your first and last name, email address, password and the branch you attend. We use them to sign you in and to show your branch’s sermons, events, news and ministries. Your password is stored securely by our service provider; nobody at the church can see it.

About you (optional)

If you choose to give them: your date of birth, gender, marital status and whether you’re a student. We use them only to add you to ministries whose requirements you meet, such as a youth or married-couples ministry. You can change or remove them at any time under Account > About you. Church staff and ministry leaders can’t see them in the app.

Ministries

The ministries you join or ask to join. Church staff and that ministry’s leaders see your name and branch so they can welcome you or answer your request.

Staff and leaders

If the church makes you staff, a ministry leader or a media leader, it records your email address for that role. News, events, sermons and photos you post for the church belong to the church.

Your location (optional)

If you allow it, the app uses your phone’s location to show how far each branch is and how long it takes to drive there. Your location is sent to Google Maps Platform to work out the routes. We don’t store it, and the app works without it.

On your phone only

Event reminders are scheduled on your phone, and settings such as the branch you chose as a guest are kept on it. They aren’t sent to us.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • The app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts the app’s accounts and data on our behalf.
  • Google Maps Platform provides branch details, photos and driving routes.
  • YouTube plays live services inside the app. Google’s privacy policy applies when you watch.

Information is sent over encrypted connections.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information until you delete your account. To delete it, open the app, tap your initial at the top right, then tap Delete account. You can also ask us by email. Deleting your account removes your name, email address, branch, the details about you, your ministry memberships and requests, and any roles you have. Copies in our service provider’s routine backups are removed when those backups expire.

Your rights

You can see and correct most of your information in the app, and delete it as described above. To ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or for any other request, email [church email address]. If you’re not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to Ghana’s Data Protection Commission.

Children

If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
